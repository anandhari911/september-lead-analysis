# september-lead-analysis
The primary objective of this project is to evaluate, optimize, and streamline the lead conversion lifecycle across all regional centers for the first half of September (Sept 1 – Sept 15).

Core Objectives & Strategic Goals
Evaluate Marketing Channel Effectiveness (ROI):

Identify which lead acquisition channels (e.g., Facebook, Website, Justdial, Walk-ins, Inbound Calls) generate the highest volume versus highest conversion quality.

Assess the performance of course-specific campaigns (such as Full-Stack Python and Data Science) to reallocate marketing spend toward high-converting sources.

Assess Branch & Regional Sales Performance:

Benchmark lead intake and processing efficiency across all 9 branch locations (Hitec City, Kukatpally, Ameerpet, Vizag, Secunderabad, etc.).

Measure regional demand patterns to identify high-performing hubs versus centers needing operational support.

Monitor Sales Counselor & BDE Productivity:

Track individual sales counselor (41 BDEs/Owners) lead management capacity, follow-up execution, and conversion efficiency.

Ensure balanced lead distribution across representatives to prevent bottlenecks.

Pipeline Optimization & Drop-Off Mitigation:

Categorize lead progression through defined funnel stages (Converted, Hot, Warm, Cold, Dropped) to pinpoint drop-off reasons.
